Team Vitality was in total domination of the tier 1 Counter-Strike 2 scene during the first half of 2026. They won first place at every event they attended; that was until IEM Atlanta 2026, a catastrophic streak-ending event. This event marked the end, not just of their 5-event consecutive 1st place streak, but also of a 21-event-long consecutive top-4 finish streak, as well as a 15-map consecutive win streak against their biggest competition, Natus Vincere, who eliminated Vitality from the event. Since IEM Atlanta, which took place in mid-May of this year, Vitality has not reached the finals at any of the five events they've attended. This has left a lot of Counter-Strike fans wondering: is this the end of an era? Research Questions: Why is Vitality's performance worse than it was during the first half of the year? Many people are comparing Vitality to Astralis, who famously dominated the Counter-Strike scene from 2018 to 2019 before their downfall. How does the current state of Vitality compare to that of Astralis during their downfall? Overpass, a map frequently played by Vitality, was removed from the map pool in early July of this year. Has the removal of Overpass impacted Vitality's performance during recent events?
