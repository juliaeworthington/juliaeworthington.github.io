Team Vitality dominated the competitive Counter-Strike 2 scene for most of 2025 and the first third of 2026. The French team stood solidly at the #1 spot on both of the Counter-Strike ranking charts, HLTV and VRS. They absolutely obliterated every team in their path. After winning two of the largest events in 2025, the BLAST.tv Austin Major 2025 and the StarLadder Budapest Major 2025, they won first place at every event they attended in 2026, becoming the first team to win two ESL Grandslams after IEM Rio 2026. This was the first time a team had shown such control of the competitive scene since the Astralis "era" of 2018 and 2019. The Danish team, which ranked #1 on HLTV for 13 consecutive months, is regarded as the greatest team to ever compete in the game's predecessor, Counter-Strike: Global Offensive. The comparisons to Astralis were numerous, among fans and professional analysts alike. Was this a new "era" of competitive Counter-Strike? But just as quickly as the "era" discussion began, it came to an end. During IEM Atlanta in mid-May, things took a turn for the worse. This event marked the end not only of Vitality's 5-event consecutive 1st-place streak, but also of a 21-event consecutive top-4 finish streak, as well as a 15-map consecutive win streak against their biggest
rival, Natus Vincere, who eliminated Vitality from the event. Since then, Vitality has not reached the finals at any of the events they've attended. Currently at the #5 spot in both HLTV and VRS standings, they have slowly fallen from their top spot over the past five months. This has left many Counter-Strike fans wondering: is this the end of an era?  

Team Vitality's downfall since IEM Atlanta is puzzling. How is a team that showed such promise during the first half of the year struggling so much during the second half? Some blame the vacations taken by players in between events. Others point to specific players. There is also the question of the map pool, which changed shortly after the event. There's even a discussion of how emotions and "tilt" factor in. While online users are calling for the team to disband or make roster changes, it is unclear what, or who, is the real problem. This project aims to analyze the performance of each of the players on Vitality's active roster: apeX, ZyWoo, Mezii, ropz, and flameZ from before and after the event. It will also look at other top 10 teams, which have shifted around significantly over the last five months. 


## Research Questions
1. Why is Vitality's performance worse than it was during the first half of the year? 
2. How does the current state of Vitality compare to that of Astralis during their downfall? 
3. Has the removal of Overpass impacted Vitality's performance during recent events?
